\begin{abstract}
University study involves moving between course materials, explanations,
practice, and decisions about what to learn next. StudyBirds is an
AI-assisted learning environment designed to connect these activities
within a persistent course context. It organizes learning around
source-linked topics, a conversational tutor, interactive activities,
and records that distinguish independent attempts from work completed
with assistance. Its broader design connects permitted learning evidence
to instructor feedback about specific course concepts. This working
draft describes the system and outlines an evaluation of instructional
correctness, study continuity, and the usefulness of instructor insights.
The central hypothesis is that linking sources, learning activities,
and evidence can make study support easier to inspect and resume.
Learning benefits and the complete instructor feedback workflow remain
to be evaluated.
\end{abstract}
