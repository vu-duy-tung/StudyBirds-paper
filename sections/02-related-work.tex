\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

Intelligent tutoring research provides a basis for studying how the
granularity of interaction and feedback relates to learning. VanLehn's
review compares human tutoring, intelligent tutoring systems, and other
tutoring systems~\cite{vanlehn2011tutoring}. Retrieval-augmented
generation combines language generation with retrieved
information~\cite{lewis2020rag}; this is relevant to the use of course
sources as context for explanations. StudyBirds brings these concerns
into a persistent study workflow, where the provenance of content and the
conditions under which an answer was produced both matter. A fuller
review should compare recent language-model tutors and instructor-facing
learning analytics before making a novelty claim.
