\section{Limitations and Ethical Considerations}
\label{sec:limitations}

Generated explanations, answer keys, and diagnoses can be wrong.
Operational walkthroughs can expose failures but cannot establish
learning gains or correctness across subjects. Progress indicators
require validation against independent evidence; activity completion
and time spent are insufficient on their own.

Study records may contain sensitive information about a learner's
difficulties. Any participant study needs an appropriate consent and
data-handling process. Instructor reporting should minimize disclosure,
consider the risk of identification in small groups, and state its
coverage and uncertainty. Students should retain control over private
study material. The intended scope is learning support and course
preparation; formal assignment submission and grading remain outside
the system.
